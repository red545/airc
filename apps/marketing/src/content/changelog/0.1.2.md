# Airc 0.1.2 public beta

Airc is an Arc-style browser with a native macOS interface on Chromium 156.0.8078.88.
This update brings easier tab organization, page captures, richer imports and everyday browsing fixes.
These notes cover changes since 0.1.0, including the changes prepared for 0.1.1.
The staged manifest records the exact build, source and checksums.

- **Mac:** Apple silicon only, macOS 14 or later. Tested on macOS 26.
- **Installing:** open the signed, notarized DMG and drag Airc to Applications.
- **Updates:** Stable checks automatically. Choose Nightly in Settings → General to try newer builds.
  Restart to Update appears when a verified update is ready. Switching back never downgrades your data.

## New since 0.1.0

- **Select several tabs.** Use ⌘-click to select tabs or ⇧-click for a range within a sidebar section.
  Move, pin, archive, duplicate or organize them together, including by dragging between Spaces and windows.
  Bulk actions support Undo; Esc clears the selection.
- **Capture a page.** Press ⇧⌘2, select an area, then copy, save or share the image. Space selects the
  visible page; ⌘-click selects an element. Capture also works in private windows.
- **Developer Mode.** Enable it in Settings → General for development pages: see the scheme, host and port,
  open the inspector, hard-reload the page and see a count of console errors.
- **Boost creation and sharing.** Start with New Boost, share a Boost file, or review one before installing it.
  Replacing an existing Boost asks first; shared JavaScript requires explicit consent.
- **Archive and backup recovery.** Clear Archive asks for confirmation and makes a recovery backup first.
  Restore from Backup lets you choose a saved sidebar backup and saves the current state before replacing it.
  Restored tabs aren't archived immediately, and daily backup cleanup keeps your manual backups.
- **More from Arc.** Import history into the matching profiles and use the extension checklist to reinstall
  extensions in the profiles that need them. Extension settings and sign-ins don't transfer automatically.
- **Set up a profile.** Sign in to Google in a new profile and choose suggested Favorites for it.
  Sign-in completes even when Google redirects at the end.
- **Links in the right Space.** Air Traffic Control rules now apply to links that would open in Peek,
  including links followed inside Peek. Use Open Link in Space to choose a destination directly.
  Ordinary in-page links keep their usual behavior.
- **More ways to use an address.** Copy URL as Quote, show an optional full-address toolbar with Show Toolbar,
  and remove a history suggestion from the Command Bar with ⇧⌫, only after you move the selection to it.
  Little Arc remembers your profile choice for each site.
- **Mini Player controls.** Collapse the player to a compact pill with Esc and expand it again without
  stopping playback. A context menu adds player actions; Space plays or pauses supported media when the player has focus.
  “–” hides the player while playback continues; bring it back from the sidebar's media tray.
  Closing the player during a call no longer silences it. Private-window Mini Player site choices aren't saved.
- **Media in the Library.** Browse images and videos downloaded in Airc and saved Captures, preview or open
  them, drag them out, or move them to the Trash with Undo. Hover over the Library button for recent files.
  Private downloads and Captures aren't listed.
- **Tidy Titles.** Settings → AI can shorten long tab titles using Apple's on-device model.
  Tabs you rename keep your names, and private pages are excluded.
- **Safari-engine media controls.** Pages that play with Safari's engine, including Spotify-type pages,
  now use the sidebar media tray, Now Playing and media keys. DRM video uses the system picture-in-picture
  window and its controls, rather than Airc's custom Mini Player. Closing a Safari-engine video in picture
  in picture pauses it and dismisses the window. Subscription playback remains untested.
- **Streaming sites inside Airc.** Spotify, Netflix, Disney+ and other supported services now open with
  Safari's engine (WebKit and FairPlay) in the same tab, split pane or Little Arc window. A one-time note
  explains the switch. **Checks were logged out only; playback with a real subscription is untested.**
  The public FairPlay test stream played successfully. Extensions don't run on these pages, and you
  need to sign in again because Chromium's logins don't carry over. Control this in Settings → Privacy →
  Play DRM sites with Safari's engine, or per site in Site Controls.
- **Private windows.** Open one with ⇧⌘N, with its own private browsing session, Find and downloads.
  Files you download remain on disk.
- **Multiple windows.** Open another with ⌘N, move a tab to a new window or drag it out of the sidebar.
  Your Spaces and tabs stay shared; showing a tab in another window moves its live page without reloading.
  ⌃⌘N opens a blank window with separate tabs. Window titles show the Space and page.
- **Tab previews.** Hover over a sidebar tab for a preview and quick actions. Turn previews off in
  Settings → Appearance → Show tab previews.
- **Shortcuts you can change.** Settings → Shortcuts lets you record, swap and reset shortcuts.
  Choose Websites first or Airc first for each supported shortcut. More familiar navigation, window and
  editing shortcuts work throughout Airc, including in page fields.
- **Save Page As.** Use ⇧⌘S to save a page with Chromium's formats; on Safari's-engine pages, use Print → Save as PDF.
- **Manage Spaces.** Reorder, rename and customize your Spaces together, including their icons, themes and profiles.
- **Sidebar on the right.** Choose Left or Right in Settings → Appearance → Sidebar position.
- **Import from other browsers.** Bring bookmarks and history from Chromium browsers, Firefox and Zen,
  or a bookmarks file. Import passwords from a CSV file you export yourself. Find these options during
  onboarding, in Settings → Import, or under Airc → Import from Another Browser.
- **Boosts.** Customize a site's colors, font and appearance, hide elements, or add your own CSS and code
  from Site Controls.
- **Chat and mail stay ready.** Pinned tabs and Favorites for supported chat and mail sites stay loaded
  when you switch away. Active call tabs also stay loaded.

## Fixes and improvements

- In the Command Bar, a result you arrow to stays selected when more suggestions arrive.
- Opening the Command Bar with ⌘T for the first time prepares its view earlier to reduce the initial delay.
- Sidebar folders, hover reveal, resizing and Space swipes have smoother transitions and more consistent
  behavior. Dragging past the minimum sidebar width hides it; pulling past the first Space opens the Library.
- Esc no longer leaves macOS fullscreen when nothing else needs it. Switching away from a page exits its
  page-level fullscreen, and private-window titlebars behave more reliably during fullscreen transitions.
- Private-window typed searches and page icons stay out of persistent caches. Hidden pages using Safari's
  engine stay loaded while that engine reports that audio is playing.

- Links opened from another app in macOS fullscreen now open Little Arc over that app, on the same
  Space. Little Arc takes the first click and moves a page into your browser window with one click.
- Reduced early idle CPU and background requests by stopping unused services and postponing the first
  routine component update check until five minutes after launch. Updates still run; downloading a newly
  installed protected-content component may take longer.
- Fixed a crash when leaving fullscreen caused by titlebar layout during the transition.
- After the initial macOS Keychain approval, Airc adopts its encryption key so later launches and
  signed updates stop asking repeatedly.
- Onboarding and Arc import have steadier layouts, clearer account choices and warnings, and imports
  merge existing data instead of duplicating it. Repeated bookmark identifiers no longer collapse entries.
- Crash reporting now records browser crashes and unexpected page, GPU and utility process exits,
  making failures easier to diagnose when anonymous reporting is enabled.
- Improved page-save error handling, password suggestion placement, and the isolation of Boosts between
  sites and documents. Opening the Command Bar no longer requests site icons while you type.
- Site Controls shows blocked pop-ups and explains when macOS is blocking a permission. Split tabs,
  the downloads card, favicons and the update button have reliability and layout fixes.

## Please know

- **Safe Browsing remains off. Do not rely on Chrome-equivalent phishing or download protection.**
- **Capture is the visible page only**, not a scrolling screenshot of a long page. Protected video may appear black.
- **Tidy Titles requires Apple Intelligence**, macOS 26 or later and a downloaded model. It is off by default;
  without a ready model, titles stay unchanged. Real-model quality is not yet fully qualified.
- **No Chrome Sync.** Signing in to Google websites is separate from browser sync, which Airc doesn't have.
- **Protected content:** Widevine can install automatically; disable it in Settings → Privacy → Play protected
  content, then reload existing tabs. Public encrypted playback passed in a disposable signed qualification app;
  Widevine certification and licensing clearance remain pending. Subscription playback is not qualified.
- **Anonymous usage data and crash reports:** on by default, never browsing content. Turn them off, or see what
  is sent, in Settings → Privacy.
- **Split View:** mixed-direction nesting is not supported.
- **Picture in picture:** it must be allowed for the site. Calls were tested with simulated media, not a real
  Meet call with screen sharing. Covering a window alone does not start picture in picture; other call sites
  do not yet have a generic fallback.
- **Passkeys:** phone/QR-code sign-in is not qualified; delayed Bluetooth permission may require a retry.
  Passkeys saved in iCloud Keychain need Apple's approval for Airc first.
- **Arc import:** unreadable encrypted logins and cookies, and partitioned cookies, are skipped.
  Google may ask you to sign in again.
- **Smoothness:** 120 Hz animation and zero idle work remain goals, not measured promises.
