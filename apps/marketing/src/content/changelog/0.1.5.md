# Airc 0.1.5 public beta

Airc is an Arc-style browser with a native macOS interface on Chromium 156.0.8078.88.
This update brings Notes sync, Easel exports and links, better tab menus, and fixes for picture in picture.
These notes cover changes since 0.1.4. The release manifest records the exact build, source and checksums.

- **Mac:** Apple silicon only, macOS 14 or later. Tested on macOS 26.
- **Installing:** open the signed, notarized DMG and drag Airc to Applications.
- **Updates:** Stable checks automatically. Choose Nightly in Settings → General to try newer builds.
  Switching back never downgrades your data.

## New since 0.1.4

- **Notes sync with your sidebar.** Pinned Notes and their pictures can sync through iCloud, encrypted with
  the rest of your sidebar. An open note follows incoming changes; conflicting edits keep a separate copy.
  On first sync, Replace explains that this Mac's pinned Notes are kept.
- **Easel exports and links.** Save a board or a selection as a PNG or an offline web page, copy it as a picture,
  or send it with Share…. Share Link… creates a read-only page you can open in another browser.
  Review what will be published first, and take the link back when you like.
- **Live Captures in Easels.** Captures can refresh from their source page and let you interact with it.
  Offscreen boards stay quiet.
- **More useful tab menus.** Move to › includes folders in the current Space. With tabs selected,
  Archive Tabs Below and Archive Other Tabs tidy the rest. The Command Bar adds Mute All Tabs and Pin to a folder.
- **Today folders stay in Today.** Arc import keeps these folders together. New Folder with N Tabs makes
  the folder in the selected tabs' own section.
- **Clearer Command Bar results.** Sites show their icons, or a coloured initial, and each row says what it is.
  Known official addresses get a check mark from Airc's own list; look-alike addresses don't.
- **Favorites show unread messages and sound.** More mail and chat sites show an unread dot, including Slack's
  count. A Favorite playing sound shows a small moving note, with a still version for Reduce Motion.
  Right-click a Favorite to turn its unread dot off.
- **Your password manager at setup.** Choose 1Password, Bitwarden, Apple Passwords or another manager,
  keep Airc's own, or skip the step. Find it again in Settings → Import.
- **Library media controls.** Filter by Images, Videos or Captures, press Space for Quick Look, and use Delete
  and Undo. Right-click the Library button to choose which recent files it shows.

## Also improved

- Quickly closing and reopening picture in picture is more reliable with both browser engines.
- Task Manager shows CPU use while a page is busy starting up.
- A link that starts Airc in Little Airc no longer opens a full browser window first. Little Airc can open over
  a full-screen app. Show Little Airc Windows and Close Little Airc Windows are in the Window menu and Command Bar.
- Airc offers a restart if its installed app has been replaced while it is running. The Updated! message stays
  centred on the page for longer and waits while you hover over it.
- App menus consistently use English. Call recorders get more compatible AppleScript tab information.

## Please know

- **Safe Browsing remains off. Do not rely on Chrome-equivalent phishing or download protection.**
- **Passkeys:** on a Mac where Touch ID isn't available (no sensor, or the lid closed), Airc may complete a
  passkey sign-in after Continue without asking for your Mac password. A fix is coming in the next update.
  iCloud Keychain passkeys still need Apple's approval.
- **Automatic updates are not proven end to end.** Checks and downloads are enabled, but the previous release's
  real update attempt stalled on the build Mac's network. The signed download is available for manual installation.
- **Cast was tested without a real receiver.** Discovery, audio/video on a TV and actual Local Network permission
  changes still need device testing. Safari-engine pages use their own AirPlay support, not Chromium Cast.
- **Easels:** export, links and Live Captures are included. Easels don't sync between Macs yet, there's no freehand
  drawing, and a shared Easel can be viewed but not edited together.
- **GitHub Live Folders:** public repositories work without an account; private and personal lists need a personal
  access token. Built-in GitHub device sign-in awaits the OAuth app setup. Live Folder settings don't sync.
- **iCloud Sync:** Notes sync was exercised with real iCloud on the build Mac. Two-Mac iCloud Keychain delivery
  remains unqualified. Today tabs stay on this Mac.
- **Library recent files:** reading a Desktop folder still needs your macOS permission. That permission journey
  across a relaunch has not been qualified.
- **Instant Links:** tested live on both engines with Google and DuckDuckGo's HTML results page, configured as
  a site search. Bing, Kagi, Ecosia and the built-in DuckDuckGo results page are untried. Claude's question
  prefill after sign-in has not been verified.
- In Arc import, the extension checklist shows one row at a time; scroll it to see the others.
- The full test suite is not green. Releases use a focused check; broader testing continues separately.
- **Capture Full Page** stops at 32,000 pixels or 48 megapixels on very long pages and says so. Protected video
  may appear black.
- **Protected content:** Widevine certification and licensing clearance remain pending. Subscription playback
  is not qualified. Disable Play protected content in Settings → Privacy if you don't want Widevine installed.
- **Anonymous usage data and crash reports:** on by default, never browsing content. Turn them off, or see what
  is sent, in Settings → Privacy.
- **No Chrome Sync.** Signing in to Google websites is separate from browser sync.
- **Arc import:** unreadable encrypted data and unsupported records are skipped with a reason.
- **Smoothness:** 120 Hz animation and zero idle work remain goals, not measured promises.
