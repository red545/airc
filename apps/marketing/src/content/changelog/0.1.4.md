# Airc 0.1.4 public beta

Airc is an Arc-style browser with a native macOS interface on Chromium 156.0.8078.88.
This update brings passkeys on this Mac, ad and tracker blocking, Cast, Easels, GitHub Live Folders,
and more of the little things from Arc. These notes cover changes since 0.1.3.
The staged manifest records the exact build, source and checksums.

- **Mac:** Apple silicon only, macOS 14 or later. Tested on macOS 26.
- **Installing:** open the signed, notarized DMG and drag Airc to Applications.
- **Updates:** Stable checks automatically. Choose Nightly in Settings → General to try newer builds.
  Switching back never downgrades your data.

## New since 0.1.3

- **Passkeys on this Mac.** Signed builds can save passkeys in your Airc profile using the Mac's secure storage.
  Each profile keeps its own credentials; deleting the profile removes its local passkeys.
- **Ad and tracker blocking.** On by default, including for existing profiles that hadn't made a choice.
  Turn it off for a site in Site Controls, or for a profile in Settings. Explicit choices are kept.
- **Cast.** View › Cast… opens a native picker for Chromecast and Google TV on your local network.
  The sidebar tray and Site Controls show the active cast and let you stop it.
- **Easels.** Press ⌃⇧E for a canvas of text, pictures, shapes, arrows, links and Captures. Arrange, resize,
  group and snap them into place. Undo survives a relaunch. Find your boards in Library › Easels.
- **GitHub Live Folders.** New Live Folder… follows a repository's pull requests, your pull requests or your
  review requests. New ones arrive; merged and closed ones leave. Keep in Folder makes a row yours to keep.
- **Tidy Tabs and folders in Today.** Group Today tabs into folders without pinning them. With Apple Intelligence
  available, click Tidy in Today or choose Tidy Tabs in the Command Bar to group them on your Mac. Tabs still
  auto-archive by their own age.
- **Share a Space or folder.** Share Space… and Share Folder… create a link that opens in an ordinary browser.
  Choose what to include, and revoke the link later. The Boost Gallery on aircbrowser.com lets you find and try
  community Boosts, and offers Update when an installed Boost has a newer revision.
- **Calls and picture in picture.** Google Meet's own picture-in-picture window gets first choice; the fallback
  has more room. Call surfaces keep one set of controls. Call chat and Share This Tab Instead are available
  where the call supports them.
- **Extensions in every profile.** Review and install an extension into the profiles you choose, with a result
  for each one. Arc import's extension checklist includes Install all.
- **Capture in more places.** Capture is in the page's right-click menu. Capture in Portrait Mode is in View
  and Site Controls: frame a page on a colour or your desktop picture. Captures can include a Note beside a page.
- **More ways to get around.** Cycle Tab Forward and Backward visit the Space's tabs in last-used order.
  ⌥⌘-click a link for Little Airc. Find and Replace (⌥⌘F) works in editable page text. AppleScript can work
  with windows, Spaces and tabs; JavaScript requires the explicit setting.
- **Your default site search.** Make any saved site search the profile's default in Settings → Site Search.
  The Command Bar uses it for ordinary searches too.
- **Ask ChatGPT.** Enable Ask AI for a profile in Settings → Site Search, then type `gpt` and press Tab in the
  Command Bar. Return opens your question on ChatGPT in a new tab. Perplexity and Claude are choices too;
  no API key or Apple Intelligence is needed. Nothing is sent until you press Return.
- **Instant Links.** Enable it in Settings → AI, then press ⇧Return on a search to preview the best result,
  or several results. Confirm to open them; if no usable result is found, Airc searches normally. It needs
  Apple Intelligence and stays off in private windows.
- **More from Arc import.** Supported site storage, partitioned cookies and additional login types now come
  across, with a result and reason for each row. A site can still ask you to sign in again.
- **Updates that wait for you.** A downloaded update waits while you're playing media, in a call or busy.
  See What's New opens its notes; automatic restarts are optional. Settings also has an app icon choice,
  sound and haptic controls.

## Also improved

- Developer Mode's toolbar has Inspect, Network, Console, and Clear data and reload. The Developer menu
  includes Task Manager and the other page tools.
- Boosts stay out of private windows; Zap works inside same-site frames.
- Notes have their own title field and can be dragged into a split.
- Site previews show recent pages for supported pinned sites and Favorites.
- Large tab restores avoid repeated sidebar work; opening the Command Bar and hiding the sidebar do less work.
- The Command Bar fixes its search placeholder and reduces the work when Tab switches into site search.
- Settings → Appearance can turn off dragging the window from the top of a page.

## Please know

- Quickly reopening picture in picture on a page that plays with Safari's engine may need a second try from the video's tab.
- In Arc import, the extension checklist shows one row at a time; scroll it to see the others.

- **Safe Browsing remains off. Do not rely on Chrome-equivalent phishing or download protection.**
- **Passkeys:** iCloud Keychain passkeys still need Apple's approval. Local secure storage and virtual WebAuthn
  tests pass; the attended Touch ID/password picker and autofill journey have not been fully qualified.
- **Cast was tested without a real receiver.** Discovery, audio/video on a TV and actual Local Network permission
  changes still need device testing. Safari-engine pages use their own AirPlay support, not Chromium Cast.
- **Easels stay on this Mac.** Live Captures, freehand drawing, export, sharing and sync are not included.
- **GitHub Live Folders:** public repositories work without an account; private and personal lists need a personal
  access token. Built-in GitHub device sign-in awaits the OAuth app setup. Live Folder settings don't sync.
- **iCloud Sync:** two-Mac iCloud Keychain delivery remains unqualified. Notes and Today tabs stay on this Mac.
- **Instant Links:** tested live on both engines with Google and DuckDuckGo's HTML results page, configured as
  a site search. Bing, Kagi, Ecosia and the built-in DuckDuckGo results page are untried. Claude's question
  prefill after sign-in has not been verified.
- **Today folders:** Arc import still brings their tabs across without the folder; New Folder with N Tabs still
  makes a pinned folder.
- The full test suite is not green.
- **Capture Full Page** stops at 32,000 pixels or 48 megapixels on very long pages and says so. Protected video
  may appear black.
- **Protected content:** Widevine certification and licensing clearance remain pending. Subscription playback
  is not qualified. Disable Play protected content in Settings → Privacy if you don't want Widevine installed.
- **Anonymous usage data and crash reports:** on by default, never browsing content. Turn them off, or see what
  is sent, in Settings → Privacy.
- **No Chrome Sync.** Signing in to Google websites is separate from browser sync.
- **Arc import:** unreadable encrypted data and unsupported records are skipped with a reason.
- **Smoothness:** 120 Hz animation and zero idle work remain goals, not measured promises.
