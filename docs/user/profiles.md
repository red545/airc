# Profiles: a new one in twenty seconds

A profile keeps its own logins, history, saved passwords and extensions. Every Space uses one profile, so a Work Space
and a Home Space can be signed in to different Google accounts side by side.

## Making a profile

Open a Space's menu (click its name in the sidebar) and choose **Set Profile › New Profile…**, or use **Settings ›
Profiles › New Profile…**.

1. **Name, icon and colour.** Type a name and pick an icon and a colour for the profile.
2. **Spaces.** Tick the Spaces that should use the new profile. Their pinned tabs and folders stay; their open tabs
   reload, signed out, in the new profile.
3. **A colour for the Space.** If you ticked a Space, the card goes on and asks for a colour for it. The window shows
   each colour as soon as you pick it. **Next** keeps it; **Skip** (or Esc) puts the Space's old colour back. You can
   change it again any time with the Space's **Theme…**.
4. **Sign in.** **Sign in to Google** opens Google's sign-in page in that Space, in the new profile, and the card moves
   out of the way while you sign in. **Another site…** opens a new tab in the Space instead, for any other account.
   **Skip** (or Esc) closes the card; the sidebar keeps its **Sign in to Google** card until you dismiss it.
5. **Done.** Once Google has signed you in, the card comes back with your account's picture and name, then closes by
   itself (or click **Done**).

Making a profile from **Create a Space › Profile › New Profile…** keeps to the one step: the new Space gets its colour in
the Create a Space card itself. A profile made in Settings without any Space skips the colour and sign-in steps.

## Signing in later, from the sidebar

A new profile's Space shows a **Sign in to Google** card at the top of Today. **Sign in** opens Google's sign-in page in
that Space. When the sign-in is done, Airc sees the account the same way the first-run setup does: it asks Google, in
that profile only, which accounts its cookies are signed in to.

Then, without a relaunch:

- The card shows the account's picture and name and offers to add **Gmail, Calendar and Drive** as Favorites for that
  account (**Add** or **Skip**), as Arc's setup offered your favourite apps.
- The account's picture replaces the profile's initial in the Space's profile indicator (beside the Space name), in
  **Set Profile ›** menus and Manage Spaces, and in **Settings › Profiles**, which also says "Signed in as …".
- Resting on a Space's icon at the bottom of the sidebar names its profile and account, e.g. "Work · Studio (Alex
  Example)", when you use more than one profile.

The profile keeps the name you gave it. The account's email and name stay on this Mac; Airc never sends them anywhere.
