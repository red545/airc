# The Library

Open the Library with **⇧⌘L**, the Library button at the bottom left of the sidebar, or a swipe past your first
Space. It slides in over the sidebar and has five sections.

## Archived Tabs

Today tabs you close, and the ones Airc archives after they've been idle, newest first by day. Click one to restore
it to its Space. Search by title or address.

**Filter** (next to the search) narrows the list the way Arc's did:

- **Where was the tab?** All Spaces, one Space, or **Little Airc**: pages from Little Airc windows archived after six
  hours.
- **How was the tab closed?** Any way, **Auto-Closed** (archived after being idle), or **Manually Closed** (⌘W, Clear,
  Archive, a deleted Space).

**Clear Archive…** (the button above the list, a row's menu, or **View → Clear Archive…**) asks Arc's "Are you sure
you'd like to Clear Archive?" with a choice: **Everything**, **Older than 7 days** or **Older than 30 days**, each with
how many tabs it removes, from every Space and profile (search and filters don't narrow it). Airc saves a recovery
backup first. A toast then says how many it cleared, with **Undo**: that puts them all back exactly as they were, once,
while the toast shows. After that they're gone; ⌘Z doesn't bring them back. (Arc cleared everything, permanently.)
**Remove from Archive…** in a row's menu removes just that one, permanently.

Clearing doesn't change how tabs get to the Archive: idle Today tabs are still archived after your profile's
**Archive tabs after** time, and archived pages older than 90 days still leave on their own (archived notes stay until
you clear them). The Archive stays on this Mac: it isn't synced, so tabs you clear can't come back from another Mac.

## Media

Pictures and videos as a grid of thumbnails, newest first, under Today, Yesterday, Earlier This Week, then each
month. Click one for Quick Look (← → move through the grid), double-click to open it, ⌘-click to show it in Finder, or
drag it out to Finder or another app. Its menu copies it or moves it to the Trash.

The picture you clicked stays selected. **Space** shows it in Quick Look or closes Quick Look, and **Delete** moves it
to the Trash. **Undo** in the bar below puts it back, and so does **⌘Z** while the Library is open, unless you're
typing in a text field.

**Filter Media** (the filter button, top right) shows only **Images**, only **Videos** or only your **Captures**. Click
the × on the filter's name to remove the filter.

**Show media from** (top right) chooses the sources:

- **Captures** you saved with ⇧⌘2, and **Downloads** from Airc's downloads list. Both are on at first.
- **Folders**: Desktop, Documents, your Downloads folder and the folder macOS saves screenshots to. Airc never reads
  them until you allow one: choosing it opens the folder in a panel, and clicking **Allow** gives Airc that folder,
  and only that folder. Airc keeps a bookmark of it, so it's still allowed after a restart. Click **Cancel** to say
  no. Airc remembers that and doesn't ask again by itself.

A folder shows only the pictures and videos at its top level: not its other files, not hidden files, and not the
folders inside it. Files you save, rename or delete in Finder show up in a second or two; Airc reads the folder in the
background. Uncheck a folder to hide its files for now and keep the permission, or choose **Stop Showing** to take the
permission back. **Hide from Library** in a picture's menu takes it off the grid and leaves the file where it is.

If an allowed folder is deleted, or you take Airc's access away in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Files and
Folders, Media says it can't read the folder any more. **Choose Again…** allows it anew.

Your media never leaves your Mac. Files from private windows never show: not their downloads, not their Captures,
even when they're saved in a folder you allowed. Airc keeps no record of those files, so after you quit Airc, a private
download left in an allowed folder can show up like any other file.

## Recent files on the Library button

Rest the pointer on the Library button to see your five newest files. Click one to open it, or drag it out to Finder
or another app. Right-click a file for **Open**, **Show in Finder**, **Copy**, **AirDrop**, **Send via iMessage** and
**Move to Trash**. After **Move to Trash**, click **Undo** on the message that appears to put the file back. While
that message shows, **⌘Z** does the same, unless you're typing in a text field or on a page: then ⌘Z undoes your
typing as usual.

Right-click the Library button to choose what the list shows (**Show media from:**): **Captures**, **Downloads**, and
**Media**, the pictures and videos from the folders Library › Media shows. These choices are separate from Media's own.

## Downloads

Every profile’s downloads and their progress, searchable, newest first.

## Notes

Every note you've written (⌃⇧N), newest edit first, wherever it is: in another Space, in a closed folder, or
archived. Search finds a note by its title or its text. Click one to open it in **Peek** over the page you're on, as
Arc's Library did: type in it right there. The expand button at the top of the Peek (or ⌘O) is **Open in Space**: the
note opens in its Space, with the folders around it opened (an archived note comes back from the Archive). ✕, Esc, ⌘W
or a click outside closes the Peek. A note's menu has **Open in Peek**, **Open in Space** and **Copy as Markdown**. A
note already on screen in another window opens in its Space instead.

## Easels

Every Easel on this Mac. A click opens it in Peek too; its menu exports it or deletes it. The card at the top,
**Export All Easels**, saves all of them as web pages in one ZIP (see [Easels](easels.md)).

## Spaces

All your Spaces, with their profile and how many tabs each has. Click one to go there, or open **Manage Spaces…**.
