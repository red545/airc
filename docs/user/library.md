# The Library

Open the Library with **⇧⌘L**, the Library button at the bottom left of the sidebar, or a swipe past your first
Space. It slides in over the sidebar and has five sections.

## Archived Tabs

Today tabs you close, and the ones Airc archives after they've been idle, newest first by day. Click one to restore
it to its Space. Search by title or address.

**Filter** (next to the search) narrows the list the way Arc's did:

- **Where was the tab?** All Spaces, one Space, or **Little Airc**: pages from Little Airc windows archived after six
  hours.
- **How was the tab closed?** Any way, **Auto-Closed** (archived after being idle), or **Manually Closed** (⌘W, Clear,
  Archive, a deleted Space).

**Clear Archive…** removes everything, after saving a backup.

## Media

Pictures and videos as a grid of thumbnails, newest first. Click one for Quick Look (← → move through the grid),
double-click to open it, ⌘-click to show it in Finder, or drag it out. Its menu copies it or moves it to the Trash
(with Undo in the bar below).

**Show media from** (top right) chooses the sources:

- **Captures** you saved with ⇧⌘2, and **Downloads** from Airc's downloads list. Both are on at first.
- **Folders**: Desktop, Documents, your Downloads folder and the folder macOS saves screenshots to. Airc never reads
  them until you allow one: choosing it opens the folder in a panel, and clicking **Allow** gives Airc that folder,
  and only that folder. Airc keeps a bookmark of it, so it's still allowed after a restart. Click **Cancel** to say
  no. Airc remembers that and doesn't ask again by itself.

A folder shows only the pictures and videos at its top level: not its other files, not hidden files, and not the
folders inside it. Files you save, rename or delete in Finder show up in a second or two; Airc reads the folder in the
background. Uncheck a folder to hide its files for now and keep the permission, or choose **Stop Showing** to take the
permission back. **Hide from Library** in a picture's menu takes it off the grid and leaves the file where it is.

If an allowed folder is deleted, or you take Airc's access away in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Files and
Folders, Media says it can't read the folder any more. **Choose Again…** allows it anew.

Your media never leaves your Mac. Files from private windows never show: not their downloads, not their Captures,
even when they're saved in a folder you allowed. Airc keeps no record of those files, so after you quit Airc, a private
download left in an allowed folder can show up like any other file.

Resting the pointer on the Library button shows your newest Captures and downloads.

## Downloads

Every profile’s downloads and their progress, searchable, newest first.

## Notes

Every note you've written (⌃⇧N), newest edit first, wherever it is: in another Space, in a closed folder, or
archived. Search finds a note by its title or its text. Click one to open it in its Space, with the folders around it
opened. An archived note comes back from the Archive. A note's menu also copies it as Markdown.

## Spaces

All your Spaces, with their profile and how many tabs each has. Click one to go there, or open **Manage Spaces…**.
