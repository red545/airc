# Send Feedback

Tell the Airc team what works, what doesn't, and what you'd like. Choose **Help › Send Feedback…** (where Arc had
Contact the Team), press ⌘T and type **feedback** (or **feed**, **bug**, **report**), or click the speech bubble at the
bottom right of the sidebar, next to the + button.

## The sidebar's Feedback button

The speech bubble sits in the sidebar's bottom row (Archive · your Spaces · speech bubble · +), just left
of +. It stays there however many Spaces you have: as in Arc, the Space icons get narrower instead (the current one
keeps its icon, the others turn into dots when room is short).
Little Airc has no sidebar, so no button. Don't want it? Right-click it and choose **Hide Feedback Button**. Turn it
back on in **Settings › Appearance › Sidebar › Show Feedback button**.

## What you write, and what you add

- **Message**: what happened, or what you'd like to see. Required.
- **Reply email**: optional. Leave it empty and no one can write back.
- **Pictures**: none until you add them, up to five. Each shows as a thumbnail; its **×** takes it away again.
  - **Paste** one with ⌘V: a screenshot you copied, a copied image, or picture files copied in Finder.
  - **Drop** picture files, or an image from a page, anywhere on the sheet.
  - **Add Screenshot…** › **Screenshot of This Window**: Airc takes a picture of the window you were in, the way it
    draws it: its sidebar and the page on it. Never your screen, another window or another app, and no
    screen-recording permission. It shows what's in that window, its tabs and the page included. A private window
    doesn't offer it (you can still paste or drop pictures there).
  - **Add Screenshot…** › **Choose File…**: PNG, JPEG or HEIC files.

  Files can be up to 5 MB each. Airc redraws each picture from its pixels before it's sent, so its location, camera,
  dates and any other details stored in the file stay on your Mac. A HEIC photo goes as a JPEG; a very large picture
  is scaled down to at most 4096 pixels across.
- **Include app info**: off until you tick it. The Airc version and build, the engine (Chromium or WebKit), the macOS
  version, the Mac's model (for example `Mac16,1`) and your locale (for example `en_AT`).

**What will be sent** lists every part as it will go, each picture with its size. Nothing else is sent: no web addresses, tabs,
history, Spaces or profiles, and nothing that names you beyond the reply address you typed.

Click **Send** (or press ⌘Return). Airc says **Thanks, it's sent** when aircbrowser.com has it.

## Offline

If Airc can't reach aircbrowser.com, it says **Saved to send later** and keeps the message on your Mac. It's sent by
itself as soon as Airc is back online (also after a restart), and a short note in the window says so. At most 20
messages wait; the oldest goes first.

## Where it goes

Feedback is stored on aircbrowser.com (Vercel Blob) for the Airc team, who get an email that it arrived. It's
deleted once it's been dealt with, or whenever you ask: write to hello@aircbrowser.com. Feedback isn't usage data
([telemetry.md](telemetry.md)): it goes only when you click Send. See the site's
[privacy page](https://aircbrowser.com/privacy#feedback).
