# What's in Airc today

Airc is a native Mac browser with Chromium for everyday browsing. Organize tabs into Spaces, each tied to a profile
with its own logins and Favorites. Pin pages and folders, search with ⌘T, restore archived tabs from the Library,
and keep several windows on the same collection of tabs. Private windows keep their browsing session separate.

You can put two to four pages in Split View, open a link in Peek, or use Little Airc for a quick lookup. Sidebar tab
previews show a picture and quick actions. The media tray controls background playback, and automatic picture in
picture can keep a video or call visible. Live Calendar, enabled in Settings → General with calendar access, adds
meeting countdowns and Join actions to a Calendar Favorite using calendars synced to your Mac.

Select several sidebar tabs with ⌘-click or ⇧-click to move, pin, archive, duplicate or group them together. The
Library's Media section shows images and videos you downloaded and Captures you saved, and resting on the Library
button shows your newest files. Clear Archive and Restore from Backup (Settings) both save a backup first. Importing
from Arc brings Spaces, pinned tabs, Favorites and history, plus a checklist for reinstalling your extensions. Copy a
page's address as Markdown or as a quote, add an optional address bar with View › Show Toolbar, and in the Mini
Player use – to hide it while playback continues (bring it back from the sidebar's media tray), ✕ to close it, and
Esc to collapse it to a small pill.

- [Protected media](protected-media.md): streaming sites use Safari's engine inside Airc, with separate sign-in
  and extension limitations. Service availability can vary; the guide distinguishes tested paths from account checks.
- [Find and Replace](find.md): ⌘F on the page, ⌥⌘F to replace in its editable fields (never a password field), with
  the page's own undo; the Services menu for selected text.
- [Command Bar](command-bar.md): ⌘T search, Site Search (a site's shortcut, then Tab), removing a suggestion with
  ⌥⌘⌫, and typing with input methods.
- [Capture](capture.md): ⇧⌘2 copies, saves or shares a picture of part of the page, with no screen-recording
  permission.
- [The Library](library.md): Archived Tabs filtered by Space, Little Airc and how they closed; Media from your Captures,
  downloads and the folders you allow; every note in one list.
- [Files and export](files.md): drop files, folders, links and text on the sidebar or use Add Files…, and export
  Spaces as Markdown or as bookmarks any browser imports.
- [Little Airc](little-arc.md): the small window for links and quick lookups, from any app with ⌥⌘N, moving a page
  into a Space, and when Little Airc windows go to the Archive.
- [Notes](notes.md): ⌃⇧N makes a note in the sidebar: headings, lists, checkboxes, links and pasted Captures, saved
  as you type, kept on this Mac; export to Markdown, RTF, PDF or HTML and import back, or have New Note open Google
  Docs, Notion, Word or Confluence.
- [Boosts](boosts.md): change a site's colors, fonts and layout, hide elements, or add your own CSS and JavaScript.
- [Space themes](themes.md): colors, light and dark, Reduce Motion and Transparency, and your theme's colors on web
  pages as Arc's `--arc-palette-*` variables.
- [AI](ai.md): optional on-device Tidy tab titles, requiring Apple Intelligence. AI link summaries and Ask on Page
  are not available yet.
- [Importing from Arc](import-from-arc.md): what comes over and into which profile, Arc open, logins and the Keychain,
  Try Again, what can't come over and why, and the extension checklist.
- [Extensions](extensions.md): Chrome extensions per profile, pinned in the URL field or at the toolbar's top right,
  and how to add them.
- [Developer Mode](devmode.md): Arc's Dev Tabs, on by itself for localhost and with ⌃D for any site: the full URL, Inspect,
  Hard Reload and a local console-call badge. Also Arc's developer menu commands (View › Developer, Clear Cookies and
  Refresh, Clear Cache and Refresh) and the Task Manager.
- [Split View](split-view.md): two to four pages in one window, pinned and Favorite splits, each pane's controls.
- [Tab and site previews](previews.md): the card on a sidebar tab, its picture and quick actions, and the pages you
  visited recently on a pinned Notion, Confluence, Figma or Linear.
- [Live Calendar](calendar.md): your next meetings on a calendar Favorite, Join, ⌃⌘J and each Favorite's settings.
- [Scripting](scripting.md): AppleScript with Arc's dictionary: windows, spaces, tabs, making and closing tabs, and
  JavaScript when you allow it.
- [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md): everyday keys, remapping and how shortcuts interact with websites; the sidebar from the
  keyboard (⌃Space), ⌘1–9's choices, holding ⌥, and VoiceOver.
- [Menus, copying and sharing](parity-p01.md): each row's right-click menu, clean links, Markdown copies, Share,
  and going back to pinned pages, also from the Command Bar.
- [Updates, app icon and sounds](updates.md): Restart to Update and what it asks about first, See What's New,
  updating while you're away, the app icon, sounds and haptics.
- [Usage data and crash reports](telemetry.md): what is shared, how to inspect it and how to turn sharing off.

No Airc account is needed. Sidebar sync and Easels are not available. These guides describe the current
implementation; features in your installed build depend on its version.
