# The Space name row

The row with a Space's name, under the Favorites, works as Arc's does.

## Folding the pinned tabs

- **Click the row** to fold the Space's pinned tabs and folders away, and click it again to bring them back. Point at
  the row and its icon becomes a chevron: it points down while the pinned tabs show and turns a quarter turn to the
  right while they're folded. The rows below slide up over the pinned ones as they fade, and slide back down when you
  open them. With **Reduce Motion** on (System Settings › Accessibility › Display), the list fades instead, and the
  chevron turns at once.
- **From the keyboard:** with the sidebar's keyboard on the name row, **←** folds and **→** opens. While the pinned
  tabs are folded, ↑ and ↓ skip them. Tabs › **Collapse Pinned Section** and **Expand Pinned Section** do the same, and
  so does the Command Bar.
- While they're folded and the tab you're looking at is one of them, the Space's name is bold, so you can tell where
  it is.
- Each Space keeps its own: fold Work and Home stays open. Every window shows a Space the same way, and Airc remembers
  it when you quit.
- **Only a Space with a name folds**, as in Arc. An unnamed Space's row has no chevron and a click on it does nothing.
  Give it a name with Rename Space… (right-click the row) to fold it. Clearing a Space's name opens its pinned tabs.

## An unnamed, only Space

If you have only one Space and it has no name and no icon, Airc hides its name row. Adding a second Space shows the
row again (and deleting it hides it), and so does giving the Space a name or an icon. Right-click the sidebar's empty
space and choose **Show Space Header** to always see it, or Hide Space Header to hide it again.

## The other states

The row shows whatever else the Space holds: with no Today tabs, or with only Favorites and no pinned tabs, it's there
and still folds (with nothing pinned, only the chevron turns).
