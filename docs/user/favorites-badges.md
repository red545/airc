# Favorites: unread dots, sound and icons

## Unread dot

A Favorite for a mail or chat site shows a small red dot in its bottom-right corner while the site has something
unread. That covers Gmail, Outlook, Slack, WhatsApp Web, X, LinkedIn, Discord, Google Messages and Linear.

Airc reads the count from the page's own title, the same text the tab already shows, as Arc did. For example
"Inbox (3)", "(12) X" or Slack's "… | 4 new items" give a dot. When the count goes away, so does the dot. Airc
doesn't read the page, your mail or your messages, and nothing is sent anywhere. A Favorite whose page isn't loaded
keeps the dot from its last title until the page loads again.

To turn the dot off for one Favorite, right-click it and uncheck **Show Unread Badge**. Check it again to bring the
dot back. Each Favorite keeps its own setting, and it stays after you quit and reopen Airc.

## X and Slack

Some sites get their own badge in the same corner instead of the dot, as in Arc:

- **X** shows its notification count on a small grey capsule (white in dark mode): "3", "20+", or "99+" for anything
  over 99. The count comes from X's own title, for example "(3) Home / X".
- **Slack** shows a red **@** when someone mentions you or writes to you directly. Slack marks that in its title with
  "!" in front. Unread channels without a mention get the plain dot.

The badge pops in when it first shows or the count goes up. With **Reduce Motion** on, it just appears.
**Show Unread Badge** turns these badges off too.

## Calendar: meeting soon

With Live Calendar on, the Calendar Favorite counts down to your next meeting ("in 20m") on a faint pill. From five
minutes before the meeting until a few minutes after it starts, the pill turns bright, so you can see at a glance that
a meeting is about to begin. The bright pill fades in, or just appears with Reduce Motion. Right-click the Calendar
Favorite and uncheck **Show Time to Next Meeting** to hide the countdown and its pill.

## Calls

A Favorite whose page is in a call, such as a Google Meet Favorite during a meeting, shows a green camera badge in its
top-right corner. A Favorite you're sharing into a call shows two green overlapping windows instead, the same sign the
shared tab's row shows. Both go away when the call ends or the sharing stops. While a Favorite is in a call or shared,
this badge takes the place of the sound badge. Airc reads this from the browser's own call state. It never looks at the
call's video or sound.

## Sound

While a Favorite's page plays sound, a small round badge with a musical note sits in its top-right corner. The note
sways gently. With **Reduce Motion** on (System Settings → Accessibility → Display), the badge stays but the note
doesn't move. A muted Favorite shows a crossed-out speaker instead. Right-click and choose **Unmute** to hear it
again. Tabs in the sidebar show the same thing as a speaker button you can click to mute or unmute.

## Icons

Right-click a Favorite, a pinned tab, a folder or a Today tab and choose **Change Icon…** to give it an emoji in
place of the site's icon. The picker's trash button brings the site's icon back.

## Private windows

Private windows don't have Favorites, so they never show an unread dot, a count, a call badge or a sound badge. Their tabs still show the
speaker button.
