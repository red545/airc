# Notes: titles and splits

Two things Arc's notes did that Airc's now do too: a title of the note's own, and a note dragged into Split View beside
a page. The rest of Notes is in [Notes](notes.md).

## The title

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open a note | The title line sits at the top, above the text. Without a title it shows the note's first line, dimmed: that's the note's name until you give it one. |
| Click the title and type | The note has that name. Its sidebar row and the window title change as you type. |
| **Return**, **Tab** or **↓** | The keyboard goes to the start of the text. **↑** on the text's first line comes back to the title. |
| **Esc** in the title | What you typed since you clicked is dropped, and the keyboard goes to the text. |
| **⌘Z** in the note | Undoes the title change as one step, with the text's own changes. **⇧⌘Z** redoes it. |
| Clear the title | The note is named by its first line again. |
| Rename a pinned note's row (double-click, or **Rename…** in its menu) | Sets the same title, so the row and the note always say the same. A name a pinned note's row had from an older Airc becomes its title the first time you open it. |
| Export | The file is named by the title, and a title that isn't already the first line goes above the text as its heading. |

A note with only a title is kept, not deleted, when you close it. Titles are one line of up to 120 characters.

## A note in a split

| Where you drop a note's row | What happens |
|---|---|
| The right or left half of a tab's row in Today | A split of the two, the note on the side you dropped it. |
| The right or left edge of the page | The note opens beside the page on screen, on that side (one more pane in a split). |
| The middle of a pinned split's row, or of a Favorite split's tile | The note joins that split as one more pane, and stays in it. |

In each case the split shows and the keyboard is in the note. ⌥-click on a note's row works as on a tab's: the note
opens in a split beside the page on screen. To take a note out again, drag its pane's handle (the top of the pane) to
Today or anywhere in the sidebar, or use **Separate All Tabs**. A split holds up to four panes, notes included.

Notes themselves still don't become Favorites, but a Favorite split can hold one. New Note in Split now also works
beside a pinned or Favorite split.

## After a relaunch

Titles, splits with notes in them (in Today, pinned and Favorites) and which pane had the keyboard all come back when
you quit and reopen Airc.

## Not yet

Notes still stay on this Mac: iCloud sync never sends a note or its title. Pinned and Favorite splits don't sync yet
either, with or without a note in them.
