# Split View

Two to four pages side by side or stacked in one window, as in Arc. Each page keeps its own Space and profile, so a
page from another Space stays signed in to its own accounts.

## Making a split

- Drag a tab from the sidebar onto another tab: the half you drop it on sets which side it goes.
- Drag a tab onto the page: the nearest edge shows where the new pane goes.
- ⌥-click a tab, pinned tab or Favorite in the sidebar: it opens beside the page on screen. ⌥-click on the tab you're
  looking at opens a copy of it beside it.
- ⌃⇧= (Add Split View), the "+" menu, or the Command Bar's Add Left, Right, Top or Bottom Split: pick a page in the
  Command Bar and it becomes the new pane on that side.
- Peek's Split button turns the Peek page into a pane.

A split holds four pages at most; adding a fifth says "Cannot Add New Pane".

## Working in a split

- Click a pane, or use ⌃⇧[ and ⌃⇧] (round the panes), ⌘[ and ⌘], or ⌃⇧1–4, to give it the keyboard. ⌘L, ⌘W, find
  and zoom act on that pane.
- Drag the gaps between panes to resize them. Near an even split they settle on it; double-click a gap to make every
  pane the same size.
- Each pane has a small handle at its top centre (point at the top of the page). Click it for the pane's controls:
  Move Left or Right (Up or Down when stacked), Add Left or Right Split, Arrange Top to Bottom or Side by Side, Make
  Panes the Same Size, Expand Current Split, Separate Tab from Split and Close Split View Pane. Drag it to take the
  pane out of the split.
- ⌃⇧− or ⌘W closes the focused pane. Separate All Tabs (the split's menu) turns every pane back into its own tab.

## Expanding a pane and separating pages

- **Expand Current Split** (Tabs menu, the pane's controls or the Command Bar): the pane you're in fills the split's
  place, and the other pages stay open behind it. Choose it again (it says **Restore Current Split** then) to get the
  split back with the same sizes, that pane still focused.
  - Giving another pane the keyboard (⌃⇧[, ⌃⇧], ⌃⇧1–4 or its part of the sidebar row) also brings the split back.
    So do adding a pane, Arrange, and showing another tab.
  - Closing one of the hidden panes keeps the expanded one as it is; closing the expanded pane shows the rest.
  - While a pane is expanded, ⌘[ and ⌘] go Back and Forward in it, as for a single page.
  - Each window has its own expanded pane. It isn't saved, and ⌘Z doesn't undo it.
- **Separate Page from Split View** (Tabs menu): the pane you're in becomes a tab of its own and stays on screen. In a
  pinned or Favorite split it becomes a pinned tab or Favorite next to the split. ⌘Z puts it back.
- **⌥-click a split's row** in the sidebar, or a Favorite split's tile, to separate it into tabs, as Separate All Tabs
  does. ⌘Z puts the split back.

## Pinned and Favorite splits

Drag a split's sidebar row into Pinned or above the Space name into Favorites. It comes back after a relaunch with each
pane at its own saved page, and a pane that wandered off can be reset like a pinned tab.

- Drag a tab onto the middle of a pinned split's row, or of a Favorite split's tile, to add it as another pane: the
  left half puts it first, the right half last. A full split takes no more.
- A Favorite split can have its own icon: right-click its tile and choose Change Icon…, or Remove Icon to show the
  pages' icons again.
- Closing, deleting or archiving a pinned or Favorite split asks "Leave site?" for every pane with unsaved changes, one
  at a time. Choosing Stay on any of them keeps the whole split open.
- Reset All Panes (the split's menu) takes every pane back to its saved page. If a pane has unsaved changes, it asks
  once first: Reset All, Cancel, or Reset Others to leave that pane as it is.
