# Little Airc

Little Airc (Arc's Little Arc) is a small window with one page, for quick lookups. Links from other apps open in it
when Settings → Links says so (the default), **⌥⌘N** opens an empty one for a new search, and a link you **⌥⌘-click**
in a page opens in one.

## Links you ⌥⌘-click

Hold **⌥⌘** while you click a link in a page, and the link opens in a new Little Airc window, as in Arc. The page you
clicked in stays as it is.

- The new window uses the page's Space and profile, so you stay signed in. This works in a Peek and in a Little Airc
  page too; a link in a private window stays in that window.
- **⌘-click** still opens the link in a background tab, and **⇧-click** still opens it in Peek.
- Turn it off with **Settings → Links → Little Airc → Open Little Airc when clicking on links with ⌥⌘ held**.
- Links from other apps don't depend on the keys you hold. **Settings → Links** decides where they open, as in Arc.

## Moving a page into a Space

- The **Open in** button at the top right moves the page into the Space it names, without reloading it. **⌘O** does
  the same.
- Its chevron (**⇧⌘O**) lists your Spaces in their colours, with ⌘O on the current one. Pick one with the arrows and
  Return, or start typing to search them. **⌥⌘O** opens the list straight into the search.
- A Space of another profile says so: the page opens again there, signed in as that profile.
- Under the Spaces are **Profile** (open the page in another profile) and **Copy Link** (⇧⌘C).

## From any app

Turn on **Settings → Links → Little Airc → Open Little Airc from any app**. Then **⌥⌘N**, as in Arc, opens a Little Airc
window from whatever app you're in. If that app is in full screen, Little Airc opens over it.

- To use another shortcut, click it in Settings and type the new one. Esc cancels; Delete puts back ⌥⌘N.
- Settings tells you if macOS or another app already uses the shortcut, so you can choose another.
- If one of Airc's own commands uses it, you can choose **Use Anyway**. The shortcut then opens Little Airc in Airc too.

With the setting off, Airc doesn't listen for any key outside its own windows.

### Looking up the selected text

**Look up the selected text** makes the shortcut search the text you've selected in the app in front. This is an
Airc addition; Arc doesn't do it.

- Reading another app's selection needs **Accessibility** access.
- Airc reads it only when you press the shortcut, and only to search for it.
- Without access, Little Airc opens empty and says so. Turn it on with **Allow…** in Settings: macOS asks once, and you
  can change it later in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

## Closing and the Archive

- A Little Airc window you leave alone goes to its Space's Archive. **Archive Little Aircs after** in Settings → Links
  sets when: after 1 hour, 6 hours (the default), 12 hours, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, or never.
- Archived Little Airc pages are in the Library's Archive under **Little Airc**.
- **Window → Close Little Airc Windows** closes all of them at once and sends their pages to the Archive the same way.
- **Window → Show Little Airc Windows** brings every Little Airc window to the front.
- While there are Little Airc windows, both are also in the Command Bar (⌘T) and in the Dock icon's menu.
- Closing a Little Airc window with a video or call playing keeps it playing in picture in picture.
